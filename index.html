MVRV: price ÷ average price paid

Under 1.0 (price below the average price paid) came before all 4 bear lows, 25–81 days ahead, with 3 false alarms. The lows themselves read higher each cycle: 0.42 (2011) → 0.56 → 0.69 → 0.75 (2022). The dashed line is that drift carried forward (about 0.82 this cycle). Lowest so far this bear: 1.10.

Puell multiple: miner income vs its yearly average

Under 0.5 = miners earning less than half their usual income. It came before all 4 bear lows (25–83 days ahead), with about one false alarm every 18 months. The lows read 0.31, 0.31, 0.39, 0.48: rising, so the line may need to move up.

Hash ribbon: 30-day ÷ 60-day hash rate

Under 1.0 = miners switching machines off ("capitulation"). It came before all 4 bear lows, but about 4 in 5 spells were not bottoms. When miners recover and price turns up (the "buy"), price was higher a year later 18 of 19 times.
